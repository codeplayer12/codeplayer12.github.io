Every lab I finish, every box I root and every detection rule I write ends up in a notes folder nobody else will ever read. That's fine for revision, but it hides the actual work. This site is where some of those notes come out into the open.

## What goes here

- **Writeups** of retired Hack The Box machines and other labs I'm allowed to publish
- **Homelab builds**: what I set up, what broke, and how I fixed it
- **Concept notes**: short explanations of things I've just learned, written so future me can follow them
- **Progress posts** for the certifications I'm working on

## What doesn't

No writeups for active HTB machines, exam content, or anything covered by a platform's NDA. If it would spoil a live challenge or break a rule, it stays in my private notes.

## Where I'm starting from

I hold Security+, Linux+, BTL1, eJPT and INE Cloud Associate, and I'm working on **CPTS**, **BTL2** and **OSCP** in parallel. The live list is on the [certs page]({{ '/certs/' | relative_url }}).
